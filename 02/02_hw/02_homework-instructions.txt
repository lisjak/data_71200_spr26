Following up on our recent class lecture on the importance and role of Regression analysis in modern statistics, this  2nd homework assignment will give you the opportunity to develop, test, and fit a number of competing regression models.  The dataset for this assignment is an abridged version of the data from the Framingham Heart Study data (see the attached Excel file and the abbreviated codebook).  The analytic assignment is to estimate and fit a series of simple (single predictor) regression models, i.e., modeling the number of cigarettes smoked per day for males and females,  modeling the same dependent variable separately for age, education and BMI—all as simple regression models. 

Once you have computed the regression analysis for all four simple models, then create a table that displays the amount of variation in the number of cigarettes smoked per day (the R^2 ) of the participants in the sample for each of the four models, and interpret the comparative power of each model for predicting smoking behavior.  You may find including a correlation matrix of the 5 variables in this study will help with your understanding of the differences among and between the models you fit to the data. 

Background readings to help with this assignment include the attached Regression Modeling Slides as well as the comprehensive treatment of Regression modeling in the e-book by Prof. James Frost.
